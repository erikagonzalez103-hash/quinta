Good to know

Questions, answered

Which class is right for me?

If you're just starting, begin with Bookkeeping 1. If your books exist but need work, Bookkeeping 2. If you file (or will file) a Schedule C and want to walk into tax season ready, take Year-End Tax Readiness with Lupe. Not sure? Any pairing works, and the bundles save you money.

Do I need any accounting background?

No. Every class is built for owners who manage their own books or work with a bookkeeper. No jargon, no assumed knowledge.

Can I come online instead of in person?

Yes. The day is at kiln Preston Hollow in Dallas, and every class is also live on Zoom. When you book, you choose how you're joining. Each class has 20 seats, in the room and online together.

Is this tax advice?

No. The classes are general education. For your own situation, talk with your tax professional.
