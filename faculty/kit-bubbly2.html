How to share it

Instagram

  1. To your Story: on Quinta’s post, tap the paper-airplane icon, then Add to story. Tap the sticker icon, choose Link, and paste your link (use Copy my link). Tap Your story.
  2. As your own post: save the image, then post it from your account and paste your caption. Links in an Instagram caption aren’t clickable, so it’s worth adding your link to your bio for the day, or using the Story link sticker above.

LinkedIn

  1. On Quinta’s post, tap Repost, then Repost with your thoughts.
  2. Paste your caption (it includes your link) and tap Post. Hashtags are optional on LinkedIn; three or four is plenty.

Facebook

  1. On Quinta’s post, tap Share, then Share to Feed (or Share now and edit).
  2. Paste your caption above the shared post and tap Post.
How to copy and paste on a phone: On this page, tap a Copy button. Then, in the app, press and hold your finger in the empty text box until a small menu appears, and tap Paste.
