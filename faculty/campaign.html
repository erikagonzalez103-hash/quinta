Where your link actually works

Not every platform lets a link travel. This is the difference between a share that pays you and one that doesn't.

  • Instagram bio — tappable. Put your referral link here for the whole campaign and leave it there. This is the one that matters most.
  • Instagram Story — tappable, via the Link sticker. Use it every time you share a post to your Story.
  • Instagram first comment — not tappable; Instagram never makes comment links clickable. Still worth posting as your first comment so people can see the address and know it's in your bio — just don't expect taps from it.
  • Instagram repost note — can't hold a link at all. That's why those scripts are 60 characters and point at your bio.
  • LinkedIn — tappable in the post itself and in the first comment. Your link is already inside every LinkedIn script below.

Every waitlist signup so far has arrived with no referral code attached. If your link isn't in your bio right now, that's the likeliest reason.
