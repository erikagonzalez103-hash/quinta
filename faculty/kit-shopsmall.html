The carousel

Quinta’s post is a carousel: nine slides, swiped in this order. Save them to your phone, then post them as your own carousel with your caption. That way your followers see every slide, and your link is in the caption.

On an iPhone or Android this opens your phone’s share menu. Tap Save 9 Images (iPhone) or Save / Photos (Android) and they’ll be in your photos, in order.

LinkedIn PDF (all 9 slides) All slides as a ZIP (for a computer)

How to share it

Instagram

  1. Save the slides with the button above.
  2. In Instagram, tap +, then Post. Tap the Select multiple icon (two overlapping squares), then tap the slides in order, 1 to 9. The numbers on the photos show the order.
  3. Tap Next twice, paste your caption (Copy caption + hashtags), and tap Share.
  4. Links in an Instagram caption aren’t clickable, so add your link to your bio for the weekend, or share the post to your Story and add a Link sticker with Copy my link. (Sharing Quinta’s carousel to your Story only shows the first slide, which is why posting it yourself is better.)

LinkedIn

  1. On LinkedIn a carousel is a document. Download the LinkedIn PDF above.
  2. Tap Start a post, then + (or More), then Add a document, and choose the PDF. Give it a title, like 15% off every class this weekend.
  3. Paste your caption (it includes your link) and tap Post. Hashtags are optional on LinkedIn; three or four is plenty.

Facebook

  1. Save the slides with the button above.
  2. Tap Photo/video, select all nine in order, paste your caption, and tap Post.
  3. Short on time? On Quinta’s post, tap Share, then Share to Feed, and paste your caption above it.
How to copy and paste on a phone: On this page, tap a Copy button. Then, in the app, press and hold your finger in the empty text box until a small menu appears, and tap Paste.
