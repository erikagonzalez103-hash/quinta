How to record your video

  1. Read your script a few times out loud. You don't need to memorize it. Say it in your own words.
  2. Hold your phone upright (tall, not sideways), at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  3. Face a window so light falls on your face. Avoid a bright window behind you.
  4. Keep your head and shoulders centered. Your video gets cropped to a square, so stay in the middle of the screen.
  5. Open your phone's Camera, switch to Video, tap the flip icon (two curved arrows) so you can see yourself, and press the red button. Aim for about 25 seconds. Press the red button again to stop. If you stumble, just start over. It's fine to record a few takes and send the best one.
  6. Upload your video to the Week 3 folder. Tap the Upload my video button below. It opens a Google Drive folder. Tap +, then Upload, and pick your video. Name it with your first name (for example, Tara).
  7. Erika sends back your finished reel, with your cards added, ready to post. It will also appear under Your reel on this page.
Upload my video

Uploading on a phone, step by step

This works best in the Google Drive app. If you don't have it, download it from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with your Google account.

  1. Tap Upload my video above. If your phone asks, choose to open it in the Drive app. You'll see a folder called Week 3 Selfie Videos.
  2. Tap the + button (bottom right corner).
  3. Tap Upload, then Photos and Videos.
  4. Pick your video and tap Upload. Leave the app open until it says the upload is finished. A video can take a minute or two.
  5. Rename it with your first name: tap the three dots next to your video, tap Rename, type your first name, and tap OK.

Can't get the upload to work? Text the video straight to Erika instead. In your Messages app, open your conversation with Erika, tap the + or the photo icon next to the text box, pick your video, and tap send.

How to copy and paste on a phone: On this page, tap a Copy button. Then, in the app, press and hold your finger in the empty text box until a small menu appears, and tap Paste.

How to post your reel

  1. Save your reel to your Photos. Tap Save my reel to Photos above, then tap Save Video. If Erika texted you a finished version, you can save that one instead: tap it in Messages, then the share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up), then Save Video.
  2. Open Instagram, tap + at the bottom of the screen, choose Reel, and pick the video.
  3. Tap Next, then tap the caption box and paste your caption (use the Copy the caption button above).
  4. Tap Share.
  5. Share each other's videos to your Story when you see them. Tap the paper-airplane icon under the video, then tap Add to story.
