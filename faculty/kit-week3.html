Post 2 · this kit

A selfie video, about 25 seconds. You read a short script to your phone. Your video sits in a square at the top, and five Fork, Femme & Foundations cards change underneath it. You only film yourself talking. Erika adds the cards and sends it back finished.

How to record your video

  1. Read your script a few times out loud. You don't need to memorize it. Say it in your own words.
  2. Hold your phone upright (tall, not sideways), at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  3. Face a window so light falls on your face. Avoid a bright window behind you.
  4. Keep your head and shoulders centered. Your video gets cropped to a square, so stay in the middle of the screen.
  5. Open your phone's Camera, switch to Video, tap the flip icon (two curved arrows) so you can see yourself, and press the red button. Aim for about 25 seconds. Press the red button again to stop. If you stumble, just start over. It's fine to record a few takes and send the best one.
  6. Text the video to Erika by Thursday, October 15. In your Messages app, open your conversation with Erika, tap the + or the photo icon next to the text box, pick your video, and tap send. She'll send it back finished, with your cards added, ready to post.
How to copy and paste on a phone: On this page, tap a Copy button. Then, in the app, press and hold your finger in the empty text box until a small menu appears, and tap Paste.

How to post it (once Erika sends it back)

  1. Save the finished video Erika sends you to your phone. In Messages, tap the video, then tap the share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) and tap Save Video. On Android, tap the three dots and tap Save.
  2. Open Instagram, tap + at the bottom of the screen, choose Reel, and pick the video.
  3. Tap Next, then tap the caption box and paste your caption (use the Copy the caption button above).
  4. Tap Share.
  5. Share each other's videos to your Story when you see them. Tap the paper-airplane icon under the video, then tap Add to story.
