Unmatched check-ins

When a student scans the class QR code, she types her name on her phone. The system then looks for that exact name on the class list to find what she paid for. When the name doesn't match — "Bobbi" instead of "Roberta," a missing last name, a typo — the scan lands here.

Her attendance is already saved. What's missing is the link to her purchase, so her account still shows this class as unused. If she bought a bundle, it looks like she has a class left that she's already taken.

What to do with each card

  • Pick the right person. Under her name you'll see everyone enrolled in that class who hasn't checked in yet. Click This is her next to the right one, and the class is marked as taken on her account.
  • Clear it if there's nothing to link. If she paid at the door, booked under someone else's name, or the scan was a test, click Clear. The scan stays in your attendance records; it just leaves this list. You can undo it right after.
