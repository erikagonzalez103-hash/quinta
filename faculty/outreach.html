Before you start

Three things that make it work

  1. Say why you thought of her. One specific line about her or her business does more than any offer. It's the difference between a message and a mass text.
  2. Point her to one class. Not the whole day. A full Wednesday is a big ask; a 10am class is not. Pick the one class that fits where she is.
  3. Invite, don't chase. Send it once, follow up once. If it's not the right time, that's a real answer, and she'll remember you were kind about it.

Script A

She's thinking about starting

She has the idea, maybe a name, maybe an Instagram handle. She hasn't filed anything yet, or she just did and isn't sure she did it right.

Where to point her

Get started, $212: Bookkeeping I and Claude for Beginners. She leaves with her books set up right from day one and a working AI habit. Or a single class: Bookkeeping I ($99), or Entity Setup ($175) if she hasn't filed yet.

If she says…

“I'm not ready yet.”That's exactly when this helps. Get started is built for before you open, not after you've set up your books the expensive way.
“I'll just Google it.”You can Google the forms. What you can't Google is which choice is right for you, and what it costs to get it wrong.
“I can't do a Wednesday.”Come for one class, in person or on Zoom. Most are about an hour and a half. And bundle classes can be taken later in the semester.
“Money's tight right now.”Totally fair. Classes start at $99, and one class that saves you a costly mistake pays for itself. If now's not it, I'll let you know when we run it again.

Script B

She's already started

She's open, she's selling, and she's been doing a lot of it by feel. She knows more than a beginner, so the pitch is the thing she's been putting off.

Where to point her

A year to three in, still finding her footingKeep the books, $168: Bookkeeping I and II. The books she'll wish she'd set up right in year one.
Established, thinking about what lastsBuild to last, $990, or one class from it: Get Certified WBE/MBE/DBE, Investing: How to Pay Yourself First, Trademarks, Legacy Planning, Brand 101.
Curious about AI, any stageThe Practice, $485: Modules 1, 2 and 3. From first conversation to her own assistant and automations.

If she says…

“I already know this.”You might! These are built for women who've been at it. The right class is the one about the thing you've been putting off.
“I'll just ask Claude.”Claude can draft the paperwork. It can't tell you what you don't know to ask. That's what the class is for, and we teach Claude too.
“I don't have a whole day.”You don't need one. Pick one class, in person or online. Bundle classes can be taken later in the semester.
“I've been meaning to get to that.”Then this is the nudge. One class, one afternoon, and it's off your list.

Works for either

The gift angle

Some women won't buy a class for themselves. They will bring a friend, or send one to someone they believe in. Use this when she deflects, or when she's the one who always champions everyone else.

The gift seat works for her too. If she wants two classes for herself, she can buy one and gift the second to her future self. Nobody checks.
